**Sora 2** is the latest creation from **OpenAI** regarding video generation.  
It is essentially a social network consisting only of **AI-generated videos**.  
These AI videos are currently flooding Czech social networks as well.

However, if you want to try it out, you will run into a problem — it is only available in the **USA**.  
Today, I will show you how to use it in the **Czech Republic**.

---

## The Web Version of Sora

Today we will focus only on the **web version of Sora**.  
The foundation is a **VPN** that has servers in the USA.  
You can use, for example, [Surfshark VPN](https://get.surfshark.net/aff_c?offer_id=926&aff_id=41789) or perhaps [Windscribe](https://windscribe.com/yo/dzno0erq),  
which is free up to a certain data limit.

After downloading your chosen VPN, activate it on one of the **American servers**.

---

## Accessing the Platform

1. Open your browser and go to [sora.com](https://sora.chatgpt.com/).  
2. Log in to your account or register.  
3. After logging in or registering, the website will prompt you to enter an **invite code**.

And here lies the problem — as of the article's release date, **Sora is only available to invited users**.  

This code can be obtained in several ways:
- Email me at [hello@adamskri.eu](mailto:hello@adamskri.eu), and if I have a spare code, I will send it to you.
- Follow the social networks **X** or **Reddit**, where users share these codes.  
  I recommend filtering posts by **newest** – finding a valid code may take some time.

---

## Generating Videos

After entering a valid code, the **Sora** platform will unlock for you,  
where you can finally start **generating your own videos**.
